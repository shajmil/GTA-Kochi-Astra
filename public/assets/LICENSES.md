## Custom assets

All vehicle, character, architectural and vegetation geometry is created in this project. Foliage masks, signs, the favicon and contact-shadow textures are generated locally. Engine, horn and ambient audio are synthesized with Web Audio. No reference photographs or third-party logo image files are redistributed.

## Fonts

DM Sans and Manrope are requested from Google Fonts, distributed by their authors under the SIL Open Font License. System sans-serif fonts are the fallback. Malayalam sign text uses the browser's installed Malayalam fallback.

Three.js and Vite are MIT-licensed; their license files are included with their npm packages. OpenStreetMap was used as a geographic reference, credited in the in-game field notes and the research notebook.
