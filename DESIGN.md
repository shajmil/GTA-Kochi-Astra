# Kochi / Afterglow

Visual thesis: a sun-warmed, humid Kochi streetscape, with weathered plaster, tropical greens, detailed transport infrastructure and a quiet editorial overlay.

Content plan: a full-canvas live world; a small city wordmark; location and journey objective; a compact map and contextual controls; an optional field guide with destinations and research credits.

Interaction thesis: smooth character and chase cameras; a gentle opening camera reveal; location title transitions and responsive destination markers. Gameplay remains the dominant visual.

## World compression

North is negative Z. Kundannoor and Maradu are south; Vyttila is north of Kundannoor and its hub sits east; Kaloor is northwest of Vyttila; Edappally is northeast of Kaloor. The bypass and a compressed urban western route form a loop. This is an authored miniature, not a map survey. The metro follows the western urban route between Vyttila, Kaloor and Edappally, not the NH66 bypass. Landmark forms are interpreted from public references.
