# Kochi reference notebook

Researched 29–30 September 2026. References informed custom geometry; photographs are not bundled or used as game textures. The roads are an authored, compressed interpretation, not imported survey geometry.

## Geographic structure

- [OpenStreetMap, Kochi corridor](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/9.9923/76.3134): inspected the north–south distribution of Maradu, Vyttila, Kaloor and Edappally. © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The miniature preserves the general sequence, puts Kaloor west of the bypass and the Mobility Hub east of Vyttila, and shortens the urban metro route dramatically.
- [Kochi Metro official station list](https://kochimetro.org/metro-stations/): Edappally → Changampuzha Park → Palarivattom → JLN Stadium → Kaloor → central Ernakulam → Vyttila. The game compresses intervening stations and represents the western urban alignment rather than placing the entire line on NH66.
- [Kerala government: Kundannoor flyover](https://keralanews.gov.in/8723/Kundannoor-Flyover-Kochi.html): the junction connects NH66, NH85 and NH966B. Modeled a multilane crossing, an elevated north–south route, ramps, support piers and service roads. Three playable flyovers stand for Kundannoor, Vyttila and Edappally.

## Landmark references and interpretation

### Forum Kochi

- [Official Forum story and Kochi photograph](https://www.forummalls.in/pages/our-story)
- [Official facade reference](https://www.forummalls.in/cdn/shop/files/kochi-1.jpg?v=1707826491)
- [Location reference](https://mapcarta.com/W1206084829)

Observed silver vertical fins, lime/green/yellow folded panels, dark side facade panels, glass ground floor, a broad canopy and a roof sign. Recreated those cues with individually modeled facade elements, planting, forecourt and an adjacent narrow canal. The miniature omits exact store names and full parking structures.

### Brototype Kochi

- [Brototype official contact/location](https://www.brototype.com/)
- [Public photo listing](https://www.justdial.com/Ernakulam/Brototype-Kochi-Kundannoor/0484PX484-X484-230607161106-D1N5_BZDET/photos)

Official address: Edathuruthikaran Holdings, 10/450-2, Kundanoor, Maradu, Ernakulam, Kerala 682304. Available reference coverage was insufficient to verify the complete exterior. The building is explicitly an approximate commercial training hub with Brototype/Brocamp signage in the correct southern area; it is not presented as an exact facade reconstruction.

### Jawaharlal Nehru International Stadium, Kaloor

- [Street-level stadium image](https://www.mappls.com/place/8SD181_1688637267858_1.png)
- [Structurae stadium entry and entrance photographs](https://structurae.net/de/bauwerke/jawaharlal-nehru-stadium-1996-kochi)
- [Kochi Metro JLN Stadium station](https://corporate.kochimetro.org/stations/jln-stadium)

Observed the oval bowl, exposed teal supports, open concourses, sweeping metal roof, cylindrical entrance cores and floodlighting. Recreated an elliptical stadium with terraces, a football field, outer supports, radial roof trusses, entrance towers and gateway, plus a nearby elevated station. Individual seat counts and full interior circulation are simplified.

### Lulu Mall, Edappally

- [Official mall overview](https://www.kochi.lulumall.in/about-us-2/)
- [Exterior photograph reference](https://getoccupi.com/malls/lulu-international-shopping-mall)
- [Additional exterior photographs](https://www.thrillophilia.com/attractions/lulu-international-shopping-mall-kochi)

Observed broad cream/gold frontage, large LuLu and hypermarket signage, retail banners and glass lower levels. Official information confirms the Edappally metro walkway and Marriott hotel campus. Represented those elements alongside the Edappally road junction and flyover. The footprint is shifted within the miniature so the playable ramp, station and mall do not overlap.

### Vyttila Mobility Hub

- [KITCO, implementing agency](https://kitco.in/services/Mobility-Hubs/1000)
- [Terminal photograph, Athulvis, Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vytila_Mobility_Hub.jpg)
- [Location reference](https://www.latlong.net/poi/vyttila-mobility-hub-473124)

The public terminal photograph was opened and visually inspected. Its repeated barrel canopies, exposed arched lattice, palm trees, bus bays and buses informed the model. The game includes six arched canopies, numbered bays, parked KSRTC-inspired and private buses, metro infrastructure, and an eastern waterside jetty. The water channel and distances are compressed scenery.

## Deliberate limits

No exact cadastral footprints, interiors, live traffic data, or current construction conditions are claimed. Shop streets, apartments, plants, vehicle designs and most street furniture are procedural. This is an exploration prototype with arcade physics; no combat, missions, vehicle damage, swimming, or police simulation is implemented.
